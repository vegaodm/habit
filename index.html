Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how HabitsLog ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app. HabitsLog is a habit-tracking app that helps you build and maintain daily routines by logging activities, setting reminders, and reviewing statistics.

The short version: HabitsLog is a fully offline, privacy-first app. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. All of your habit data stays on your device.

2. Information We Collect

HabitsLog collects no personal data. Specifically:

  • No account system — we do not require you to sign up, log in, or provide an email address.
  • No tracking or analytics — we do not use analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers.
  • No crash reporting — we do not collect crash logs or diagnostic telemetry.
  • No third-party services — the app communicates with no backend servers and has no network dependency.

3. Your Habit Data

3.1 Local storage

All data you create — habits, check-in entries, notes, durations, and reminders — is stored exclusively on your device in a local SQLite database inside the app's sandbox (Application Support/HabitsLog). Nothing is transmitted to us or to any remote server.

3.2 Import & export

HabitsLog offers manual backup and restore using .sqlite files. Export writes a copy of your database to a file you choose; import lets you replace your local data with a backup. These operations are performed by you through iOS' document picker, and any file you share is under your control. We cannot access or recover your backup files.

3.3 Deletion

You can delete individual entries, habits, or the entire app from your device at any time. When you delete a habit, it is moved to a temporary Deleted section in the app, where you can restore it or permanently remove it. Habits are only erased from your device when you run a permanent delete, when you delete the app, or when you overwrite your data by importing a backup. Because we hold no copy of your data, deletion is immediate and irreversible.

4. Notifications and Device Permissions

  • Notifications — with your permission, HabitsLog schedules local reminders to help you build habits. Notifications are generated on your device and are never sent to any server. You can manage or disable them at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Files — when you import or export a backup, iOS presents a document picker so you can choose the file. We do not scan or access other files on your device.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

HabitsLog offers an optional auto-renewing subscription ("HabitsLog Member") that unlocks unlimited active habits, the app in additional languages (Spanish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Korean, German, and French), and importing backups that contain more than 8 active habits. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, subject to Apple's privacy and payment policies. HabitsLog never receives or stores your payment card details, billing information, or any purchase transaction data, and your chosen language stays on your device.

  • Payment is charged to your App Store account.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in Settings > Your Name > Subscriptions on your device.
  • Terms of Use (EULA): https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

6. Children's Privacy

HabitsLog is suitable for general audiences. Because the app does not collect any personal information, no special provisions are required for children under the applicable data protection regulations.

7. Security

Your data is protected by iOS' built-in app sandbox and device security (e.g., passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). Since all data remains on-device, there is no data in transit or at rest on external servers to secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected in the app.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at:

HabitsLog no recopila datos personales. En concreto:

Todos los datos que usted crea — hábitos, registros de asistencia, notas, duraciones y recordatorios — se almacenan exclusivamente en su dispositivo en una base de datos SQLite local dentro del sandbox de la aplicación (Application Support/HabitsLog). HabitsLog ofrece una copia de seguridad y restauración manual mediante archivos .sqlite. La exportación escribe una copia de su base de datos en un archivo que usted elige; la importación le permite reemplazar sus datos locales con una copia de seguridad. Dado que no conservamos ninguna copia de sus datos, la eliminación es inmediata e irreversible.
